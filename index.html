05 · THE WRITING WORKSHOP

Get the application read

A Hill office reads a cover letter and a one-page memo before it reads a transcript. The Workshop helps with both.

  1. Pick three openingsUse the filters above to find roles that match your policy area and your level.
  2. Draft the sampleMost offices ask for a short memo, a letter to a constituent or a press release.
  3. Bring it to a coachBook a Writing Fellow before you send it.
  4. Apply and trackFollow each posting's instructions exactly; offices screen on that first.
